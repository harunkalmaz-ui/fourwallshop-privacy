FourWallShop Pinterest Automation – Privacy Policy

This application is used by FourWallShop to automate the publishing of original wallpaper product content from its Etsy shop to its own Pinterest business account.

The application may access the authenticated FourWallShop Pinterest account’s Pins and Boards solely for the purpose of creating, managing, and linking Pins to FourWallShop product listings.

The application does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. It is not intended for use by general users and does not access other users’ Pinterest accounts.

Authentication credentials and access tokens are used only to enable authorized Pinterest API access for the FourWallShop account.

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact FourWallShop through the Etsy shop contact system.
